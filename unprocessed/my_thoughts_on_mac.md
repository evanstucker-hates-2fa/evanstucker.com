This is specifically for my 2022 Macbook Pro with MacOS 13.4

# Things I like about Mac

1. `cat rappers_delight.txt | say`
1. Homebrew reminds me of AUR.
1. Apple Silicon seems pretty efficient regarding power.
1. Macbook Pro is quiet.

# Things I hate about Mac

1. You have to click everything twice: once to get focus, and a second time to actually click the button.
1. Magic mouse has no middle click for pasting.
1. Magic mouse scrolls left and right accidentally.
1. Docker/Podman have to run in QEMU - they aren't native.
1. It's closed source.
1. Copy and paste are wrong.
1. Apple doesn't fucking understand keyboard layout standards:
1. Backspace is called "Delete", and there is no backspace.
1. Home and End keys are missing, and if you plug in a keyboard that has them, they don't work right.
1. PgUp and PgDn keys are missing, and if you plug in a keyboard that has them, you will be unpleasantly surprised.
1. "Command"/Super key is in the wrong fucking place - it should be between Ctrl and Alt.
1. Alt is named Option.
1. Some shortcuts like going to the beginning and end of a line require you to press Mac "Function" key, which doesn't exist on standard keyboards.
1. There's an "Eject" key that does nothing anymore, because they don't even include disc drives in their machines.
1. Windows don't snap neatly to left, right, or full screen when you drag them to edges of the screen.
1. Application keyboard shortcuts are fucked - e.g. in Firefox Ctrl-F5 doesn't refresh, it's "Command"-R instead.
1. Apple Silicon is probably good, but it's still lacking support.
1. No Ethernet jack
1. No USB-A ports
1. No HDMI or DisplayPort
1. No replacible battery
1. Bad value for what you get.
1. Logo is used food instead of a sweet-ass Penguin.
1. Mac Terminal sucks - mostly because of aforementioned Home/End/PgUp/PgDn problems. Workaround is to use iTerm2...
1. Magic Mouse scrolls sideways constantly - very annoying.
1. Magic Mouse scrolls up and down a little if my palm touches it in the wrong way.
1. It never powers off, and it's always connected to bluetooth headphones.
1. For real? Shift-Command-4 is how you screenshot on a fucking Mac? In know I simply press the PrintScreen key and select which kind of screenshot I want.
1. You can only command-tab to the active window of an application. For example, if I have two Firefox windows open, command-tab doesn't show both of them. This was a training error: apparently you can press down arrow to see windows of an app.
1. where the fuck is the `-w` option for the `killall` command?
1. https://stackoverflow.com/questions/8766730/tar-command-in-mac-os-x-adding-hidden-files-why
1. Mac `date` doesn't work. You have to `brew install coreutils` to get `gdate` and then alias `date` to `gdate`.
1. Pressing "Delete" key doesn't delete files... why would it? That would be too convenient.
1. Apple, against not keeping up with standards, doesn't support OWE wifi yet...
1. Every day, MacOS decides that I'm a light-mode motherfucker, when, in reality, I'm dark mode 24x7x365.
1. MacOS display scaling is blurry and shitty unless you use APPLE BRAND (R) (TM) brand fucking RETINA (TM) (R) displays. Horseshit.
1. Apple Music cannot be completely uninstalled from a Mac, but you can prevent it from launching automatically by using a third-party app called NoTunes. This app stops Apple Music from opening when you connect Bluetooth devices or press media keys.
